How your details are used

We store your name, flat number and email only to tell you when a parcel arrives. Only the concierge team at your building can see them, and they're never shared or used for marketing.

Records of collected parcels are deleted after 90 days. Your details are deleted when you move out or ask us to remove them.

To update or remove your details, contact the concierge desk.
